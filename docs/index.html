Talk to us
Where do you work?+

We are based in Killerrig, County Carlow, and serve Carlow and the South East. Our stated coverage includes Kilkenny, Wexford, Waterford, Wicklow, Kildare and Laois. Contact us with your location to confirm availability.

How do I get a quote?+

Call, email or send a WhatsApp enquiry with your location, the service you need and a few details about the property. You can attach photos in WhatsApp. Your quote is confirmed after we review the job.

Do you clean homes and business premises?+

Yes. Dolmen provides exterior cleaning for both residential and commercial properties, from individual cleaning jobs to ongoing maintenance.

Can I arrange regular cleaning?+

Yes, you can discuss regular window cleaning or planned exterior maintenance. The right frequency depends on your property and surroundings.

Is pressure washing suitable for every surface?+

Different materials need different methods. Hard landscaping may suit power washing, while more delicate surfaces can require soft washing. We assess the surface before recommending the approach.

How often should gutters be cleaned?+

An annual clean is a useful starting point, often after autumn leaf fall. Nearby trees and recurring blockages may mean your property needs more frequent attention.

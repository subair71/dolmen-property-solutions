How the quote form works on this website.

Quote form

The quote form prepares a message in your browser. This website does not store submitted form details in a database or send them automatically.

Contacting Dolmen

When you choose Continue to WhatsApp, the details you entered are included in a WhatsApp link. You decide whether to send the message. WhatsApp handles data under its own privacy policy. Calling or emailing uses your device’s corresponding application.

Photos and personal information

Only include information needed to describe the job. You can add photos in WhatsApp; avoid unnecessary personal information or images of other people.

Analytics and external websites

No marketing analytics or advertising cookies have been added to this website. Links to Instagram, Google Maps, Golden Pages and WhatsApp take you to services with their own privacy practices. Hosting providers may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site.
